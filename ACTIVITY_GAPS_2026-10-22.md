## Best additions and where they fit

### 1. Shanghai: UnTour Street Eats Breakfast

**My strongest food recommendation.** Guided neighborhood eating adds something different from another restaurant reservation: breakfast stalls, market browsing, and a local park.

- Published: **RMB 525/person, 8–11 a.m., approximately 2.3 km walking**, meeting near South Shaanxi Road metro. Public groups have a maximum of 12; a private departure requires paying for at least eight adult tickets.
- Best slot: **Monday November 23 morning**, if keeping the Shanghai city/spa day. This replaces the slow morning and leaves time before the 3 p.m. spa. It does not fit a Suzhou/Wuzhen/Hangzhou day trip that same day.
- From Atour X: plan a separate journey into the former French Concession; confirm the actual meeting point before routing.
- Evidence: UnTour has a large, strongly positive TripAdvisor review base, approximately 4.9/5. Do not confuse the operator rating with one specific departure.

[Operator details and direct price](https://untourfoodtours.com/shanghai-street-eats-breakfast/) · [Chinese TripAdvisor reviews](https://cn.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g308272-d2013889-Reviews-UnTour_Shanghai_Food_Tours-Shanghai.html)

### 2. Hong Kong: Aqua Luna red-sail harbour cruise

**Best easy evening upgrade near the Salisbury.** A seated cruise with a drink is a different experience from the practical Star Ferry crossing, although both cover the harbour.

- Published: **45 minutes, HK$280/adult, one drink included**. Tsim Sha Tsui Pier 1 is convenient for your hotel.
- Candidate: **Sunday November 15, 5:30 p.m. Tsim Sha Tsui round trip**, returning around 6:15 before your confirmed 7 p.m. Hutong dinner. Replace part of the ferry/waterfront block; keep a buffer for disembarkation and getting upstairs at Hutong.
- Select the correct pier and departure: some sailings drop passengers across the harbour. November seats still need checking. Tickets are nonrefundable under the published terms.
- TripAdvisor snapshot: approximately **4.4/5**, with hundreds of reviews. Choose it for the boat and skyline rather than expecting the light show to carry the evening.

[Bilingual operator timetable](https://aqualuna.com.hk/experiences/evening-harbour-cruise/) · [Traveler reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g294217-d2039777-Reviews-Aqua_Luna-Hong_Kong.html)

### 3. Taipei: 郭元益 pineapple-cake baking workshop

**Best short craft/food experience.** Make the pastry yourself and take cakes home; a useful alternative to another shopping or museum block.

- Chinese Klook listing: **two hours**, Shilin branch, **4F, 546 Wenlin Road**. It displayed **NT$640** before selecting a date/package; treat that as an indicative listing price, not a November quote.
- Evidence: **4.9/5 from 314 Klook reviews** in the retrieved listing, including 2026 reviews. The listing offers Chinese, English, and Japanese; confirm the language for the chosen session.
- Best slot: **Saturday November 28 morning**, if choosing the urban finale. Replace a daytime option; do not combine it with a full north-coast excursion. Leave Thanksgiving family time and Friday's Palace Museum/Beitou plan alone.
- From San Want: requires a journey north to Shilin. Exact session time and November availability remain unverified.

[Chinese listing, reviews, and booking options](https://www.klook.com/zh-TW/activity/57963-taipei-kuo-yuan-ye-foods-mango-crisp-diy-experience-diy-experience/) · [Official Chinese course page](https://www.kuos.com/zh-TW/pages/pineapple-cake-diy-introduction)

### 4. Shanghai: Maglev as part of the Pudong arrival transfer

**Best small addition for the technology interest.** Make the airport journey part of the experience instead of allocating another sightseeing block.

- Use **Friday November 20**, after the scheduled 2:20 p.m. Pudong arrival: PVG → Longyang Road, then metro or taxi to the Bund hotel.
- Shanghai Airport's Chinese page lists **RMB 50 ordinary single fare** and **300 km/h operation**. Older traveler reviews advertising 431 km/h are not a reliable promise of today's service.
- It is not door to door. With luggage, delays, or tired travelers, a direct taxi may be more comfortable. Your November 25 departure uses **Hongqiao**, so save this for Pudong arrival.
- TripAdvisor snapshot: approximately **4.6/5**, with thousands of reviews.

[Shanghai Airport Chinese Maglev information](https://www.shairport.com/cxf/index.html) · [Traveler reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g308272-d9594458-Reviews-Maglev_Transportation-Shanghai.html)

### 5. Hong Kong: Sha Tin race afternoon — a deliberate swap

**Most distinctive new event option.** The Hong Kong Jockey Club's November calendar lists a **daytime Sha Tin meeting on Saturday November 14**. The famous Wednesday Happy Valley night meeting on November 18 falls after you leave Hong Kong.

- This would replace a substantial part of Central/Peak on November 14. Allow a half day including transport from Tsim Sha Tsui; this is not a nearby one-hour stop.
- Worth considering if the group likes a lively local sporting occasion more than another viewpoint. Keep the existing sightseeing plan if racing itself does not appeal.
- TripAdvisor snapshot: approximately **4.4/5**, with hundreds of reviews. First-race time, admission arrangements, and any reserved-seat inventory need checking separately.

[Official dated fixture calendar](https://racing.hkjc.com/en-us/local/information/fixture?CalMonth=11&CalYear=2026) · [Traveler reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g294217-d593132-Reviews-Sha_Tin_Racecourse-Hong_Kong.html)

### 6. Chongqing: 湖广会馆 Huguang Guild Hall — a compact alternative

**Best modest cultural gap.** Historic courtyards, carved woodwork, assembly halls, and migration history offer a change from skyline viewpoints and crowded commercial streets. The municipal archives identifies the site at **东水门正街4号**.

- Planning allowance: **60–90 minutes**, plus the journey from the Jiefangbei hotel. Combine with the eastern old-city area when nearby.
- Best opportunity: **November 19 if the robot visit does not happen**, or replace an alley stop on November 18. Do not squeeze it between a distant Shui Tu robot appointment and the tentative 5 p.m. BBQ.
- TripAdvisor snapshot: **4.1/5 from roughly 67 reviews**. This is a pleasant optional stop, not evidence that the trip is missing a major must-see.
- Current admission and November performance times were not established. Do not assume a regular opera performance comes with entry.

[Chinese municipal history and location](https://jda.cq.gov.cn/web/article/web/content_1461428557456216064.html) · [Traveler reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g294213-d1814784-Reviews-Chongqing_Huguang_Assembly_Hall-Chongqing.html)

- **Chongqing:** pandas, Liziba, Hongya Cave exterior, alleys, cableway, south-bank walks, river cruise, Ciqikou, teahouse options, Chongqing 1949/Sichuan opera, and major out-of-town alternatives are already present.
- **Shanghai:** Bund, French Concession, Yu Garden, museums, acrobatics, spa, robot interest, water towns/day trips, and bars/cabaret already have coverage.
- **Taipei:** Palace Museum, Beitou, night markets, TaipeiEYE, north coast, Yingge pottery, Maokong, and mountain options are already included.

Disneyland and Ocean Park are prominent in traveler rankings, but each would need a full-day tradeoff. They are not automatic additions to this already full trip. Likewise, do not treat a municipality-wide Chongqing ranking as proof that distant Wulong or Dazu is a quick downtown outing.

## My recommended choices

Prioritize **Shanghai breakfast tour + Taipei pineapple-cake baking**, add **Aqua Luna** if the November 15 timing works, and make **Maglev** an optional arrival transfer. Decide on **Sha Tin instead of part of Central/Peak** only if racing appeals. Keep Chongqing's guild hall and escalator as optional substitutions.

This adds variety while protecting confirmed dinners, Thanksgiving family time, and the existing travel buffers. None of these activities has been booked or inserted into the itinerary.
